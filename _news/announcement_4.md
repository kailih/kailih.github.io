Excited to share our latest work, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00638">It Takes Two to Match: Co-Evolving Generative Retriever with Reinforcement Learning</a>. We introduce CoGR, a generative retrieval framework in which LLMs generate matching keywords for both queries and items, and the two sides co-evolve through reinforcement learning.
