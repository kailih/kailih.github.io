Excited to share that our paper, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14903">ColBERT-serve: Efficient Multi-Stage Memory-Mapped Scoring</a>, was accepted and presented at the 47th European Conference on Information Retrieval (<a href="https://ecir2025.eu/">ECIR 2025</a>). The work introduces a memory-mapped multi-stage scoring approach that makes late-interaction neural retrieval (ColBERT) far more efficient and scalable for serving LLM-powered retrieval systems.
